About ATS Resume Screener

This is a simple web application that helps job seekers analyze their resumes against job descriptions to improve chances of passing Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

Features include:

  • Upload your resume and paste the job description to get a compatibility score.
  • Create a simple ATS-friendly resume using our online form.
  • Download professionally designed ATS-friendly resume templates.
  • Tips and suggestions to improve your resume formatting and content.

Built with Python Flask and Bootstrap.
